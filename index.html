Where it started
16
The age the school put me on the payroll

My mates went back for their A-levels. I went back on the payroll.

I got into computers in a big way at school, and it only grew from there. At sixteen, while my friends returned to sit their A-levels, I returned too — but hired by the school to help run their IT systems.

I never really left. To this day I still support school IT systems through my company, Integrated Networks. It's been the through-line of the whole twenty-five years — always hands-on, always in the real systems people actually use.
